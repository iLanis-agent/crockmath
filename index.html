Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Crock Math does conversion and volume arithmetic with labeled kitchen norms - 30 oven minutes to about 2 hours on high or 4.5 on low, the half-to-three-quarter fill band, 1.5 cups of stew per serving. Your cooker's own manual wins every tie. This page never judges doneness or food safety; follow your recipe's guidance.

The conversion truth

Every 30 minutes of oven simmering becomes about 2 hours on high or 4.5 hours on low. An hour of oven time is 4.0 hours on high or 9.0 on low - which is why Tuesday's stew starts before work, not after.

The fill truth

A slow cooker works best half to three-quarters full. Three quarts of stew in a 6.0-quart cooker sits right in the band. Two quarts runs hot and fast - check it early. Five crowds the lid.

The serving truth

A serving of stew is about 1.5 cups, so 6 servings ask 9.0 cups - 2.3 quarts. Count the eaters first and the cooker size picks itself.
